Buying notes

Check JoyaGoo product links

Open the source listing and record the marketplace, seller, item ID, color code and size. A Taobao, Weidian or 1688 listing can reuse the same cover image for several variants. Compare the photo tied to your exact option and check the seller’s exchange deadline before ordering.

Compare sizes in centimeters

Measure a piece you own that fits well. Use flat chest width for hoodies and jerseys, shoulder width and sleeve length for jackets, and inseam for shorts. For matching sets, check both size charts. For sneakers, use heel-to-toe length and confirm whether the chart gives foot length or insole length.

Read JoyaGoo QC photos

Quality-control photos are warehouse images you can use to inspect a specific item before dispatch. Match the color and size label, compare both shoes in a pair, and zoom in on zipper alignment or stitching. If the scale is unclear, ask for a photo with a ruler beside the item.

Estimate JoyaGoo shipping costs

Compare the item subtotal, domestic delivery, service fees and international postage in one currency. For destinations such as the US, UK or EU, check the carrier’s rules for tracking and whether the quote includes destination charges. Use the same packed dimensions and destination when comparing carriers.

Frequently asked questions

What is a JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that groups product links by category and may include sizing notes, seller details or warehouse photos.

Where do the category links go?

The category links open the corresponding CAFANS catalog pages. This guide is independent of JoyaGoo and the brands shown.

Can QC photos prove that a branded item is authentic?

QC photos can show visible condition and help you match a variant. For authenticity, look for an authorized seller and verifiable proof of purchase.

What should I do if a product link expires?

Search the original marketplace using the seller name or item ID. Confirm the replacement listing’s photos, size chart and variant before using it.

Why can the final shipping quote change?

The packed dimensions, destination and carrier can change the quote. Compare actual weight and volumetric weight once the package has been measured.
